How ROBINMAP staking rewards work

A total pool of 900,000,000 $RMAP is allocated to staking rewards. Each reward epoch distributes $RMAP according to eligible staking weight, so your share depends on how much eligible ROBINMAP you have staked and how long it remains staked during the epoch.

  • The 900M $RMAP pool is dedicated to staking emissions.
  • ROBINLAND is a separate in-game reward and does not reduce your $RMAP allocation.
  • ROBINLAND rarity is assigned from five tiers: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, and Legendary.
  • Final land drop odds and game-balance parameters will be published before staking goes live.
