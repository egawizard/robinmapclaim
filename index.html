Rather than randomizing traits off-chain, ROBINMAP reads coordinates directly out of Robinhood Chain's genesis block. Each piece's position, palette, and rarity tier are hash-derived — meaning the collection's layout existed the moment the chain did.

Every mint is checkable against a public Merkle proof, so ownership and trait authenticity can be verified independently of the mint site at any time.
